# WEEK 13 DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT — FAMILY SHIELD
**Target length:** 3 minutes 30 seconds  
**Recording status:** Script only. A live screen recording must still be made by the student.

## 0:00–0:25 — Context
“Family Shield is a family preparedness tool for Mexican parents who may receive an urgent call that appears to come from an adult child. Voice cloning means a familiar voice is no longer enough to prove identity. Our product does not detect deepfakes. It helps the family pause and verify through another known channel.”

## 0:25–1:10 — Home and pause
“On the home screen, the user sees ‘Pausa antes de pagar.’ I start a verification. The first instruction is simple: do not send money under pressure. End the call if the caller is rushing you, and check through a different channel.”

## 1:10–2:00 — Contact order
“The app reminds the user to call the relative using a number already saved, not a number supplied by the caller. If that does not work, the family follows its pre-agreed order and tries two trusted contacts. The app does not access the contact book or make calls.”

## 2:00–2:40 — Outcomes and limits
“If the user independently confirms the situation, the app records only that the person reports a separate check. This is not an authenticity guarantee or payment authorization. If they cannot confirm, the app enters the pause protocol: do not transfer while the request remains unverified.”

## 2:40–3:00 — Feedback mechanism
“The prototype logs anonymous event types and timestamps locally in the browser. It does not collect names, numbers, voice recordings, messages, or bank data. The log can be cleared.”

## 3:00–3:30 — What changed my mind this week
“What changed my mind is that a safety flow can still create false confidence if its language is unclear. The biggest risk is not a missing feature; it is a user thinking the app has verified a caller. I simplified the language and made the backup-contact step explicit. The next step is to test this with five real users, including at least three strangers, and change the product based on what they actually do.”
