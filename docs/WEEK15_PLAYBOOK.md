## 1. Strategy
Audience: Mexican parents/caregivers (initial focus 45–65) coordinating with an adult child living elsewhere; secondary audience: adult children and trusted relatives. Moment: an urgent call or voice note appears to come from a relative and asks for money. Need: a clear next action under fear and time pressure. Position: family preparedness guide for pausing and checking through a separate known channel. Promise: “Pausa antes de pagar. Comprueba por otro medio.” Evidence: existing prototype implements pause → saved-number callback → trusted contact 1 → trusted contact 2 → pause fallback. This proves the flow exists, not that it prevents fraud or is understood. Never promise deepfake detection, caller authentication, emergency certainty, fraud prevention, or payment control.

## 2. Brand system
Voice rules: (1) calm, not alarmist; (2) direct everyday Mexican Spanish; (3) honest about limits. Weak line: “Detecta si una voz es falsa con IA.” Strong line: “Si una llamada te pide dinero con urgencia, haz una pausa y comprueba por otro medio.”
Palette: forest green #1E3A2F; warm ivory #F7F7F1; sage #EAF1EB; ochre #C58B43; ink #26352D. Type: Georgia/serif display headline; DM Sans/Arial body and controls. Hierarchy: H1 36–48 px; H2 22–28 px; body 16–18 px; CTA 16 px semibold. Repeatable layout: logo/series → one headline → 1–3 actions → visible limitation → one CTA. Mexico fit: Spanish first, familiar family situations, WhatsApp-ready formats, printable checklist, large type and low reading burden.

## 3. Six concepts / three series
A — Reconoce el riesgo: A1 “Una voz conocida no es una verificación.” A2 “La urgencia es una razón para pausar.”
B — Actúa en el momento: B1 “Pausa antes de pagar.” B2 “Si no contesta, ¿qué sigue?” Show family → trusted contact 1 → trusted contact 2 → pause.
C — Prepara a tu familia: C1 “Acuerden hoy dos contactos de confianza.” C2 “Practiquen el plan en cinco minutos.”

### Asset 3 — WhatsApp family checklist
PLAN FAMILIAR: PAUSA · LLAMA · COMPRUEBA
Before an emergency: agree on two trusted contacts and their order; choose a private family phrase as an additional signal, never the only proof; save each person’s usual number; practice what to do if the first person does not answer.
If an urgent call asks for money: (1) PAUSE. Do not transfer under pressure or share codes. (2) HANG UP if pressured and call the number already saved. (3) IF NO ANSWER, contact trusted person 1, then trusted person 2. (4) IF YOU CANNOT VERIFY, keep the pause. Seek help through a known channel; if there is a credible threat, contact authorities through official channels.
Footer: “Family Shield no verifica identidades, no detecta deepfakes y no bloquea pagos. La decisión siempre es tuya.”

## 5. Creative process — input → method → output
Research: audience problem + official sources → fact sheet; check source/date and no invented statistics. Writing: fact sheet + audience + goal → Spanish copy; check clear action and non-alarmist language. Visual production: approved copy + brand system → carousel/video/checklist; check legibility, contrast, captions, mobile format. Evidence/claim check: final claims → claim ledger; every factual claim supported and limitations visible. Style review: draft asset → brand-consistent version; three voice rules met. Revision: test observations → revised asset; reproduce confusion and verify correction. Human approval before publication.

## 6. Distribution test
Primary channel: WhatsApp family sharing; secondary: Instagram carousel and Reel. Test Asset 1 carousel and Asset 2 Reel with five target users; include parents/caregivers 45–65 and adult children; recruit at least three strangers where feasible. Show without explanation. Ask: What would you do? Which number would you call? What if nobody answers? What can Family Shield do/not do? Would you share it? Record participant codes, consent, comprehension, hesitation, completion, and voluntary next action only. A false belief that the app verifies callers is a stop-and-revise result. CTA: “Comparte este método con tu familia” / “Prueba el protocolo gratuito.” Do not claim a waitlist unless it exists.
