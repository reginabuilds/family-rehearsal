# WEEK 15 DEMO SCRIPT — FAMILY SHIELD
Required recording: 3:00 walkthrough + 0:30 reflection. Use fictional demo content only.

0:00–0:25 — Introduce: “Family Shield helps Mexican families prepare for urgent calls that appear to come from a relative and ask for money. It does not detect cloned voices or verify callers. It gives families a simple method to pause and check through another known channel.”
0:25–0:55 — Show /distribution. Explain first audience, promise, and three series: recognize risk, act in the moment, prepare your family.
0:55–1:35 — Open live protocol. Start a fictional verification. Show pause and saved-number callback. State that the app does not make calls or access contacts.
1:35–2:15 — Show trusted contact 1, then contact 2. If no check is possible, show “No se pudo comprobar. Mantén la pausa.” Explain that the product never authorizes or blocks payment.
2:15–2:45 — Show carousel, Reel script, WhatsApp checklist, and five-person comprehension test. Do not report invented results.
2:45–3:00 — State which checks were actually run and which remain pending.
3:00–3:30 — Reflection: “What changed my mind is that content must not make people think the app itself verifies a caller. The strongest promise is a repeatable family method, not AI detection. The constraint is that real audience response and comprehension still need to be observed. My next step is to test the carousel and video with five target users, record what they actually do, and revise the biggest misunderstanding.”

Recording checklist: show actual live URL; use fictional data; show no personal contacts/messages/phone numbers; keep safety limitation visible; export MP4 and check audio/text readability.
